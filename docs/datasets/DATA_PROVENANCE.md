# Data provenance

Download date: 5 November 2026. The machine-readable copy is `data/manifests/source_manifest.json`, regenerated by `python -m safe_time_evrp.data.download` and checked by `python -m safe_time_evrp.data.verify`. Raw files are gitignored.

## EVRPTW-ARC (intended primary source)

- Record: [10.5281/zenodo.22800776](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22800776), queried at `https://zenodo.org/api/records/22800776`.
- Title on the record: Realistic-EVRPTW: An OSM-Enriched Benchmark for Energy-Aware Electric Vehicle Routing with Time Windows. Version V1. Publication date on the record: 2026-09-19.
- Creators: Nada Morgham and Mariem Belhor, Laboratoire des technologies innovantes.
- License field on the record: Apache-2.0.
- Access: `restricted`. The record's file list was empty. `GET /api/records/22800776/files` returned HTTP 403.
- Download status: `access_restricted`. No archive was saved under `data/raw/evrptw_arc` and labeled as this dataset.
- What the record text does say, and what this project does not assume: the 92 Schneider instances are mapped onto the Lille road network, with directed distance, travel time, slope, elevation, road type, WLTC speed profiles, and crossed scenarios of driving style, traffic, and temperature. The record presents those fields as inputs to an energy model. It does not say that the files already contain calibrated electric-truck kilowatt-hours. This repository does not invent those kilowatt-hours.

## Schneider E-VRPTW (operational foundation)

- Deposit: Dominik Goeke, E-VRPTW instances, Mendeley Data, version 1, published 2019-04-15. DOI: [10.17632/h3mrm5dhxw.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/h3mrm5dhxw.1).
- API used: `https://data.mendeley.com/public-api/datasets/h3mrm5dhxw`.
- License: CC BY-NC 3.0. [https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0). Non-commercial use only. The instances remain the authors' data.
- File: `evrptw_instances.zip`, 116,406 bytes.
- Publisher SHA-256 and computed SHA-256: `54e305a03cc0aa8aba085d3e7fefc4e3357aba34357e0cd1f84c5b36c6c1df02`.
- Extraction: 93 files (92 instances and `readme.txt`), 680,840 uncompressed bytes, path-traversal and size checks in `safe_extract`. The zip is unchanged.
- Limitation: these files do not contain Lille slopes, temperatures, or WLTC profiles. Those attributes are absent, not filled in.
- Parser result: 92 instances, 5,960 customer rows in total, zero parser failures. Families: 29 names starting with `c`, 35 with `r` but not `rc`, 28 with `rc`. Size tokens: 56 full `_21` instances with 100 customers, and 12 each of the 5-, 10-, and 15-customer files.

The deposited readme states that distances are Euclidean, that `Q` is the energy capacity, `C` the cargo capacity, `r` the energy per unit distance, `g` the time per unit of energy recharged, and `v` the constant speed. The parser follows that text.

## KU Leuven BEV energy dynamics (calibration source only)

- DOI: [10.48804/8KPDTW](https://doi.org/10.48804/8KPDTW). API: `https://rdr.kuleuven.be/api/datasets/:persistentId/?persistentId=doi:10.48804/8KPDTW`.
- Version 2, released 2025-09-04. License on the record: CC BY 4.0. The V2 readme states the same license and points to the paper by Yasko, Moussa, Tian, Kazmi, Driesen, and Martinez.
- Authors on the record: Mohamed Aboubacar Yasko, Adamou Moussa Issaka, Fanghao Tian, Hussain Syed Kazmi, Johan Driesen, and Wilmar Martinez Martinez.
- `readme_V2.txt`, 2,004 bytes. Published MD5 `af00ec7244a6680beed61d34d4a8ed9b`. Computed SHA-256 `fcd84aa6fc26f31f753889ba323b7a92d532426fbe5b06f174d51a5936206c46`.
- `BEV energy dynamic data_V2.zip`, 162,310,975 bytes. Published MD5 `cc2d63758b411c0f8d4b61bdaa1fff1c`. Computed SHA-256 `97bcf4b914cd7312a08be49d53ae704a2450d39faa64d0c94d1294cd8eb92016`.
- Extraction: 2,319 files, about 1.40 GB uncompressed. The archive was extracted with the same traversal and size limits. It was not converted into routing energies.
- Role: later calibration and plausibility checks. It is not an EVRP instance and it is not the same vehicle or the same routes as Schneider or EVRPTW-ARC.

## Transformations

Schneider and KU Leuven zips were extracted and left otherwise unchanged. No customer was dropped by the parser. The development episode uses all five customers of `c101C5`; it does not silently discard rows. Release times are a separate scenario.
